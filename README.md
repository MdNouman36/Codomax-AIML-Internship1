## 1. Basics of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML)
* **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** A branch of computer science focused on building systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and decision-making.
* **Machine Learning (ML):** A subset of AI that enables systems to automatically learn patterns from historical data and improve their performance over time without being explicitly programmed for every rule.

---

## 2. Difference Between AI, ML, and Deep Learning

| Feature | Artificial Intelligence (AI) | Machine Learning (ML) | Deep Learning (DL) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scope** | Broadest umbrella concept | Subset of AI | Specialized subset of ML |
| **Core Mechanism** | Rule-based logic, search algorithms, and statistical models | Statistical algorithms (Linear Regression, Decision Trees, SVM) | Multi-layered Artificial Neural Networks (ANN, CNN, RNN) |
| **Data Dependency** | Works with structured rules or small datasets | Performs well with moderate amounts of structured data | Requires massive datasets for high accuracy |
| **Feature Engineering** | Manual rule creation | Requires manual feature extraction by developers | Automatically extracts features through hidden layers |
| **Hardware Needs** | Standard CPUs | Standard CPUs | High-performance GPUs / TPUs |
